History

The origin source: Wikipedia

Kvass in Russia appeared in the X-XI centuries. The word "kvass" itself is also of Old Russian origin. Apart from Russia kvass is prepared in Belarus, Serbia, Macedonia, Slovakia and many other countries, but it is called everywhere the same - kvass.

Classification source: Wikipedia

According to Russian GOST for industrial production is a beverage with a volume fraction of ethyl alcohol not It is a beverage made as a result of unfinished alcoholic and lactic fermentation of wort.
